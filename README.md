One image summing up the world's news of **Monday 23 November 2026**:
29 dated stories across 7 regions, led by Nvidia reinstating Jensen Huang
as CEO — shares up 31%, adding $952bn in a day — with Modi's 24,000-crore
Nepal package and Moscow's warning to Estonia as the other pictured leads.

Every story on the page happened, or was first reported, on Monday
23 November 2026 (UTC). Anything from 22 November or earlier — the COP31
collapse, the Libya ship strike, the Las Vegas GP, the Hong Kong polls,
the Bahrain demands, the Dymer strike, Skubal's $450m Mets deal, the
Democrats reaching 258 House seats, the Kryvyi Rih toll, the "Chapo
Isidro" arrest, Bolsonaro's ICU admission, the Bayelsa boat capsizing — is
named in the DATE CHECK box as deliberately left out, not presented as
Monday's news. Claims carry their label (CONFIRMED / OFFICIAL / REPORTED /
CLAIM / FORECAST) and their source. The timeline is chronological in UTC,
and the page states "corrections: none" plus its time-zone cut-off.

The image is hand-drawn with Pillow (no photos); `main.py` (Python 3.13,
standard library only) copies it to `/output/entry.png`, deterministically.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
